# Welcome to Petroni Lab!

[We are a research group](https://petronilab.org/) at the [European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL Rome)](https://www.embl.org/groups/petroni).

We build **AI-driven systems for scientific discovery** that combine multimodal reasoning, agentic exploration, tool usage, knowledge retrieval, and lab-in-the-loop interaction.

Our research group established the foundational [Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.11401) framework. Today, we go beyond static retrieval by engineering autonomous, tool-using AI agents capable of dynamically selecting specialised biological tools, executing code, cross-referencing multi-omics data with scientific literature, and collaborating with scientists in real time.

---

### 🔬 Core Research Pillars

- 🤖 **Agentic Scientific Discovery** — Autonomous AI agents that reason, plan, invoke tools, and iterate to formulate and test biological hypotheses.
- 📚 **Knowledge Retrieval & RAG** — Structuring and grounding large language models in verified biomedical knowledge bases and massive literature corpora.
- 🧪 **Lab-in-the-Loop AI** — Embedding intelligent agents directly into wet-lab experimental workflows to monitor live data and optimize experiments in real time.
- 📐 **Scientific Benchmarks** — Designing challenging, realistic evaluation suites to test AI models on complex multi-step scientific reasoning.
